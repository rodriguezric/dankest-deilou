# AFTER THE VIOLET EYE

You wake to sunlight.
Warm, ordinary, golden.

The bell is quiet.
The air smells of bread and rain.

Outside—
fields, bright and green.
No walls.
No maze.
Only wind combing the wheat.

Birdsong.
Distant laughter.
The river keeps its own small promises.

You breathe.
The world answers.

Your eyes wander—
beyond the mills, past the meadows…

There, at the rim of the day,
clouds gather like closed fists.
Shadows lengthen where noon should be.

On the far horizon,
something rises—

a bruise of light,
round and patient,

the moon… turning violet again.
