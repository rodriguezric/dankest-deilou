The moon turned purple.
The bell tolled thirteen.

By morning, the fields were gone—
a labyrinth stood in their place.

Its walls breathed a cold mist.
Its paths bent the sun.
Something inside… listened.

Strange days followed.
Wells tasted of salt.
Shadows walked ahead.
Children drew spirals they didn’t remember.

At dusk, the maze shivered.
A scrap of parchment drifted out:
“I am older than forgetting.”

Latchford could not ignore it.
The council posted a notice:

“Adventurers, scholars, sword and spell—
enter the labyrinth.
Find its heart.
Remove it… or seal it.”

By moonrise, the square was full.
Cloaks, armor, quiet hopes.

At the edge of town, an open mouth of stone waits,
breathing like a word about to begin.

Your story starts here.
